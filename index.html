The four sales methods

FOUR WAYS TO FIND YOUR CUSTOMERS

01

DOOR-TO-DOOR

Visit transport companies, bus operators, delivery businesses, schools, mines and other organisations that operate vehicles.

02

FACEBOOK AND WHATSAPP

Use social media, Facebook groups, Marketplace, WhatsApp Status and direct messages to find interested customers.

03

COLD CALLING

Contact businesses that operate vehicles, introduce Wazambi GPS and arrange a proper follow-up conversation.

04

REFERRALS AND CONNECTIONS

Speak to vehicle owners, friends, relatives and business contacts who can introduce you to potential customers.

HOW YOU EARN

YOU FIND THE CUSTOMER. WE INSTALL THE GPS. YOU EARN COMMISSION.

STEP 1

You find an individual or business that needs GPS tracking.

STEP 2

You register the customer using your unique Wazambi Agent Code.

STEP 3

The customer pays Wazambi GPS, and our team completes the installation.

STEP 4

After payment and installation are verified, your commission is recorded in your agent account.

EARN UP TO K7,200

Reach 30 verified vehicle installations to unlock your commission for withdrawal. Your recorded commission remains saved while you work toward the target.

The amount earned depends on completed, paid and verified installations.
